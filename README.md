# Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun

![Iruma-kun Poster](https://your-direct-image-link.jpg)

## About
**Genre:** Fantasy, Comedy, Supernatural  
**Original Creator:** Osamu Nishi  
**Anime Studio:** Bandai Namco Pictures  
**Anime Premiere:** 2019  

*Fourteen-year-old Suzuki Iruma is sold by his parents to a demon, who adopts him as his grandson and enrolls him in a school for demons. Iruma has to hide that he's human while trying to survive and make friends.*

## Main Characters
- **Suzuki Iruma** – the kind-hearted human protagonist
- **Asmodeus Alice** – Iruma's loyal friend and classmate
- **Valac Clara** – a cheerful and mischievous classmate
- **Sullivan** – Iruma's demon grandfather and the school's chairman
  - He adopts Iruma and spoils him a lot
